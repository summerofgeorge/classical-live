# ClassicalWatch: U.S. conservatory coverage audit

Checked September 28, 2026 against the [Wikipedia list supplied by George](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_conservatories_of_music_in_the_United_States). The fetched page contains **82 institution entries**; the Anderson explanatory note is not a school. Every entry is accounted for below. Wikipedia is the discovery inventory, not proof that a school is active or that a performance is streamed.

**Results:** 8 new automated sources; 20 existing automated sources; 4 existing browser-reviewed sources. The remaining entries are candidates, restricted programs, outside the classical focus, or historical/closed institutions. This does not claim that every school or concert is available.

The eight additions initially returned 43 retained listings in the rolling collection window (including today/recent listings preserved by the calendar). Counts change with publication and cancellation. Free public streaming must be stated by the event or by a sufficiently specific school policy. Physical ticket prices, free campus admission, an undated video player and archived recordings are not substitutes.

Bard, Lamont, Syracuse Setnor, Cincinnati CCM and Oregon are the strongest next investigations. Bard now has dated 2026–27 noon-concert streaming announcements, but reliable calendar access and a current public viewing destination still need confirmation. Lamont posts some links only 24 hours ahead. These are follow-up leads, not enabled collectors or newly scheduled monitoring.

“Not verified in this pass” means the reviewed official pages did not establish an eligible current stream; it does not mean the school never streams. Existing manual observations retain their original verification dates. This report stays out of the public site’s About section.
